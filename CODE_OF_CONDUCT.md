# Contributor Covenant code of conduct

## Our pledge

Members, contributors, and leaders pledge to keep this community free from
harassment for everyone. This pledge applies regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community
through our actions and interactions.

## Our standards

You help create a positive environment when you do the following:

- Treat other people with empathy and kindness.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it respectfully.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes. Apologize to affected people and learn
  from the experience.
- Consider the interests of the whole community as well as individuals.

You must not engage in the following behavior:

- Use sexualized language or imagery, or direct sexual attention or advances
  toward others.
- Troll, insult, make derogatory comments, or attack others personally or politically.
- Harass others publicly or privately.
- Publish another person's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Engage in other conduct that someone could reasonably consider inappropriate
  in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards. They take fair,
appropriate corrective action for behavior they judge inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and duty to remove, edit, or reject
contributions that violate this code of conduct. This includes comments,
commits, code, wiki edits, and issues. Leaders explain moderation decisions
when appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all community spaces. It also applies when you
officially represent the community in public, including these situations:

- You use an official email address.
- You post through an official social media account.
- You act as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders use the following guidelines to decide the consequences of
violations. They consider the behavior and its effect on the community.

### 1. Correction

This response applies to inappropriate language or other behavior that leaders
judge unprofessional or unwelcome.

Community leaders send a private written warning. The warning describes the
violation and explains why the behavior was inappropriate. Leaders may request
a public apology.

### 2. Warning

This response applies to a violation through one incident or a series of actions.

Community leaders issue a warning that states the consequences of continued
behavior. For a specified period, you must not interact with the people involved.
This includes unsolicited contact with the leaders who enforce this code.
The restriction applies in community spaces and external channels, such as
social media. Violating it can lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

This response applies to a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

Community leaders prohibit all community interaction and public communication
for a specified period. During that period, you must not contact the people
involved publicly or privately. This includes unsolicited contact with the
leaders who enforce this code. Violating the ban can lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

This response applies to a pattern of violations. Examples include sustained
inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or
disparagement of groups of people.

Community leaders permanently prohibit public interaction within the community.
